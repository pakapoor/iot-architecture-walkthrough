WHY TEMPORAL HERE
🎯 One ticket per incident
Duplicate alerts land on the same workflow ID, so they update the open ticket instead of creating new ones.
🔁 Retries that survive outages
Workflow state is durable. Workers crash, ServiceNow goes down for hours — it resumes exactly where it stopped.
⏰ Escalation is code, not cron
"No ack in 30 min → page L2" is a timer inside the workflow. No polling jobs, no scheduler tables.
✅ Closes its own loop
Device comes back → Flink signals the workflow → ticket auto-resolves, with an audit trail of every step.
Before: SQS → DLQ → handler + cron for retries and escalation  ·  After: one durable workflow owns the whole incident lifecycle
